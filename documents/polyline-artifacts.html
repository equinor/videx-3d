Polyline Geometric Defects & Artifacts 12 Topologies

Visual catalog and formal taxonomy of pathological geometric features along 2D & 3D polylines.

1. Cusp / Needle Reversal
Hairpin, 180° Turn-back, U-turn
Angular
Two consecutive segments reverse heading almost entirely over a near-zero arc length.
Hazard: Tangent vector vanishes or flips; extrusions self-intersect and turn inside out.
Remedy: Turn-angle threshold detector, chord bridging, or minimum turn-radius clamp.
2. Kink / Elbow
Dog-leg, Sharp Break, Acute Corner
Angular
Sharp angular discontinuity violating minimum bend radius (e.g. Dogleg Severity in wellbores).
Hazard: Severe geometry pinching, mechanical constraint violations, visual creases.
Remedy: Chaikin fillet, circular arc blending, or Catmull-Rom smoothing.
3. Notch / Dent
Bayonet Step, Inset Nock, Square Jog
Angular
Localized rectangular or triangular indent that steps off and immediately returns to the trajectory.
Hazard: Creates dual overlapping self-intersections when offset outward or inward.
Remedy: Morphological opening, Douglas-Peucker simplification, convex hull bridge.
4. Loop / Pigtail
Bowtie, Figure-8 Knot, Self-Cut
Topological
A polyline that intersects its own previous segments, creating an enclosed interior pocket.
Hazard: Reverses polygon winding (CW ↔ CCW), corrupts polygon boolean cuts and triangulation.
Remedy: Bentley-Ottmann line-sweep intersection knot untying.
5. Fold / Accordion
Pleat, Doubling-Back, Collinear Overlap
Topological
Segments double-back along the same collinear path, traversing the same space multiple times.
Hazard: Zero-area degenerate triangles, severe Z-fighting, raycasting / picking corruption.
Remedy: Monotonic arc-length projection filter, collinear edge collapsing.
6. Overhang / Undercut
Monotonicity Inversion, S-Bend Hook
Topological
Path reverses its progression along the dominant reference axis (e.g. well trajectory vs field axis).
Hazard: Breaks 2.5D column carving, heightfields, and single-valued surface drape passes.
Remedy: Monotonic curve segmentation, 3D parametric coordinate mapping.
7. Spike / Whisker
Barb, Thorn, Rogue Outlier Point
Sampling
A single isolated vertex displaced far perpendicular to the local trajectory (sensor glitch).
Hazard: Inflates bounding boxes, corrupts camera frustum culling, generates needle geometry.
Remedy: Median outlier filter, Laplacian position smoothing, Ramer-Douglas-Peucker.
8. Micro-Chatter / Jitter
Sawtooth Noise, High-Freq Ripples
Sampling
Rapid, alternating small-amplitude oscillations caused by numerical truncation or sensor noise.
Hazard: Normal vectors oscillate violently; noisy shading, specular sparkles, miter explosion.
Remedy: Moving average window, Savitzky-Golay filter, Gaussian kernel convolution.
9. Staircase / Aliasing
Taxicab Stepping, Grid Quantization
Sampling
Discretization artifacts from rasterization or integer grid snapping producing 90° right angles.
Hazard: 10x vertex inflation, jagged lighting normals, unnecessary triangle count.
Remedy: Visvalingam-Whyatt area-based decimation, linear regression segment merge.
10. Swallowtail Pinch
Offset Loop, Self-Swallow, Caustic Pinch
Offset
Occurs when offsetting a concave curve inward at distance $d > R_{\text{curvature}}$.
Hazard: Creates self-overlapping ribbon geometry and inverted face winding.
Remedy: Polygon clipping (Clipper2 / Vatti algorithm), Medial Axis Transform.
11. Miter Spike / Horn
Beak, Corner Blowout, Acute Miter
Offset
Miter join length diverges to infinity ($d = w / \sin(\theta/2)$) at acute turn angles.
Hazard: Giant geometric spikes pierce nearby geometry, fences, or casing envelopes.
Remedy: Miter-limit clamp with automatic bevel / round fallback.
12. Coincident Duplicates
Zero-Length Segment, Vertex Clump
Sampling
Consecutive vertices located at identical or sub-epsilon distance coordinates.
Hazard: Division by zero when computing unit tangent $\mathbf{T} = \Delta \mathbf{p}/\|\Delta \mathbf{p}\| \to \text{NaN}$.
Remedy: Strict distance deduplication pass (`dedupePolyline2D/3D`).
